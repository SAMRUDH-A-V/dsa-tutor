LINKED LISTS

Reverse a Linked List:
Given the head of a singly linked list, reverse the list and return the new head.

Brute force approach:
Store the nodes in an ArrayList and reconnect them in reverse order.
Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(n)

Optimal approach:
Use three pointers: prev, current, and next.
Reverse the current node's next pointer and move all three pointers forward.
Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(1)

Common mistakes:
1. Losing the next node before changing current.next.
2. Returning the old head instead of the new head.
3. Forgetting to move the pointers forward.

Hint progression:
Hint 1: What pointers do you need to remember while traversing?
Hint 2: Before changing current.next, where should you save the next node?
Hint 3: Can you use prev, current, and next to reverse each link?
